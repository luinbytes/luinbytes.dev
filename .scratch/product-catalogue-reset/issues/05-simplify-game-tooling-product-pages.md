# 05 - Simplify Game Tooling Product Pages

**Parent:** .scratch/product-catalogue-reset/spec.md

**What to build:** Compact bespoke product pages for Risk of Anticheat, BrcTrainer, DaggerFall, and SuperHackerGolf that clearly explain and link each tool.

**Blocked by:** Browser Regression Harness

**Status:** ready-for-agent

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Replace the long Risk of Anticheat, BrcTrainer, DaggerFall, and SuperHackerGolf case-study presentations with compact pages on their existing dedicated routes.
- [ ] Give each page a hero plus visible `What it does`, `Under the hood`, and `Get it` sections.
- [ ] Keep all product descriptions and metadata factual and verify source and installation destinations rather than inferring them.
- [ ] Retain an individual visual identity for each product while using the shared site shell and quality bar; do not flatten them into one generic template.
- [ ] Ensure each page works at desktop and mobile sizes without overflow or unusable content.
- [ ] Add focused browser coverage for successful route rendering and all required visible sections.
